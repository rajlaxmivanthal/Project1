# 🌿 The Golden Spoon – Heritage Forest Restaurant

**The Golden Spoon** is a beautiful heritage-themed restaurant website inspired by the natural beauty of **Araku Valley**, traditional Indian food culture, greenery, and peaceful dining experiences.

The website combines a **vintage aesthetic** with a modern and responsive design to create a unique digital experience for couples, friends, and families.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Golden Spoon is designed as a forest-inspired restaurant website where **nature, food, tradition, and modern dining** come together.

The concept takes inspiration from the forests, hills, and indigenous food traditions associated with the **Araku Valley region**. The website highlights ingredients and traditional cooking ideas such as millets, seasonal greens, bamboo cooking, and mountain coffee.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Create an attractive restaurant website using HTML and CSS.
* Present traditional and heritage-inspired food.
* Showcase the natural beauty and atmosphere of Araku Valley.
* Provide a peaceful and vintage-style visual experience.
* Display restaurant facilities and dining experiences.
* Provide a table reservation section.
* Make the website responsive for different screen sizes.

## ✨ Main Features

### 🏡 Home / Hero Section

The homepage introduces **The Golden Spoon** with a forest-inspired background, heritage styling, and buttons for exploring the menu and reserving a table.

### 🌲 Araku Valley Inspiration

A dedicated section presents the restaurant's connection with the Eastern Ghats and Araku Valley-inspired food traditions.

Featured concepts include:

* 🌾 Millets
* 🌿 Forest Greens
* 🎋 Bamboo Cooking
* ☕ Mountain Coffee
* 🍲 Slow Food

### 🍛 Heritage Menu

The website includes a traditional food menu with dishes such as:

* Ragi Mudde & Village Dal
* Samala Pulihora
* Seasonal Forest Greens
* Bamboo-Cooked Chicken
* Stone-Pot Dal
* Mountain Coffee
* Korra Village Bowl
* Korra Payasam
* Millet & Nut Sweet

The menu is presented using image-based cards with descriptions and heritage-style labels.

### 🌙 Dining Experiences

The website provides different dining moods:

* **The Moonlit Garden** – peaceful setting for couples.
* **The Forest Lounge** – relaxed space for friends.
* **The Heritage Courtyard** – family-friendly dining atmosphere.

### 🌿 Greenery & Facilities

The website highlights:

* Garden Dining
* Warm Lighting
* Natural Wood
* Peaceful Corners

These features support the forest and heritage theme of the restaurant.

### 🏺 Ancient Food Wisdom

The project represents traditional food concepts through:

* Clay
* Bamboo
* Millets
* Seasonal Food
* Slow Cooking
* Nature

### 🧼 Hygiene Section

The website also focuses on cleanliness and modern food-safety practices.

Features include:

* Clean Dining
* Kitchen Hygiene
* Clean Tableware
* Fresh Ingredients

### 📖 Restaurant Story

The story section presents The Golden Spoon as more than a restaurant, bringing together **food, nature, heritage, and people**.

### ⭐ Guest Reviews

A guest review section is included to showcase customer experiences and the restaurant's atmosphere.

### 📅 Table Reservation

A reservation section allows visitors to enter their details and request a table.
