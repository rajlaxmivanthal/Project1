WELCOME TO THE FOREST TABLE

Where nature becomes part of the meal.

Step away from the noise and enter a restaurant surrounded by the feeling of a forest retreat.

Deep greens, natural wood, soft golden lighting and heritage-inspired interiors create a peaceful setting for couples, friends and families.

Our menu celebrates traditional ingredients, slow cooking and recipes inspired by the diverse food cultures of India.

INSPIRED BY THE EASTERN GHATS

A Taste of Araku

Our concept draws inspiration from the forests, hills and indigenous food traditions associated with the Araku Valley region.

Traditional ingredients such as millets, seasonal greens, bamboo cooking and locally inspired coffee become part of our heritage dining experience.
